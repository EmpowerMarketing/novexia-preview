About Novexia

A pharmaceutical partner built on trust.

Novexia, Inc., doing business as Novexia Biopharma, supplies pharmaceutical products and medical devices to the businesses that care for patients.

Who we are

Focused on the professional supply chain.

Novexia Biopharma is a U.S. pharmaceutical company headquartered in Valley Stream, New York. We work primarily business-to-business, supplying licensed retail, independent and chain pharmacies, wholesale buyers, clinics and other qualified healthcare organizations.

Our portfolio spans injectable and non-injectable peptides, medical devices, and a growing pipeline of prescription and OTC products for domestic trade. We also own REBORN, a premium consumer brand, starting with REBORN Hearing Aid Glasses.

We're building a modular portfolio, so new products, package configurations and labeling can be added as they're approved and launched.

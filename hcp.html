Eligibility & licensing

Who can purchase.

Professional products are supplied only to licensed, qualified purchasers. We verify credentials before sharing restricted documentation or opening ordering.

  • State-licensed retail, independent and chain pharmacies
  • Licensed wholesalers and distribution partners
  • Clinics, medical practices and outsourcing facilities
  • Other qualified healthcare organizations, case by case
